# Article review notes

The four numbered Markdown files contain edited articles and companion LinkedIn
teasers. The same article content is stored in `content/site.json` for the website.
`linkedin-teasers.md` collects all four teasers in one place.

The original argument and examples are retained. Repeated headings, editor UI
text, duplicated author text, and image controls from the exports are omitted.
No personal anecdotes, measured results, or citations have been invented.

Editorial changes to review:

- Article 1 retains the original price ranges but labels them illustrative.
  No vendor prices were independently checked. The deployments are not assumed
  to provide equivalent capacity. The claim that inference is free after hardware
  purchase is replaced with a distinction between API charges and operating costs.
- Article 2 treats the 12-month-to-12-week launch target as an example, rather
  than an achieved result. Modernization and delivery capacity are discussed
  without implying that parallel work can never succeed.
- Article 3 qualifies local-model accuracy, latency, security, hardware limits,
  and licensing. High-stakes model output still needs review. These qualifications
  are editorial clarifications, not results from a benchmark.
- Article 4 retains the proposed Hermes/OPA/handoff/n8n/OpenClaw architecture.
  The stray “Sağlık ve Yaşam” phrase in the source is removed; it conflicts with
  the runtime identified elsewhere in the article. The edit explicitly separates
  OPA's decision from enforcement, and explains that an agent's risk label does
  not grant authority. This is a proposed architecture, not a claim of a tested
  integration between those products.

Dates are retained from the LinkedIn PDFs. Articles and teasers have not been
published or posted. Their full-article URLs will work after deployment.
